> SAMPLE procedure for demonstration. Replace with your airport's approved SOPs.

## Immediate actions
1. Verify on camera whether the person wears a valid staff pass or uniform. Do not assume intent.
2. Dispatch the nearest security officer to the staff-only area.
3. Keep the person in view on the camera and note direction of movement.

## Challenge procedure
- Approach calmly and politely ask for identification and the reason for being in the area.
- Escort anyone without authorisation out of the area. Do not use force except to prevent harm.
- Check whether any doors or access points were left open or tailgated.

## Escalation
- If the person refuses to leave, is aggressive, or accessed airside areas, call the police and the security control room.
- Review access logs and CCTV for how the person entered, and report the breach to the duty manager.
