> SAMPLE procedure for demonstration. Replace with your airport's approved SOPs.

## Immediate actions
1. Do not touch, move or open the bag. Keep passengers calm and do not announce a security threat.
2. Confirm the location on the floor map and check the nearest camera view for the owner.
3. Dispatch a security officer to observe the bag from a safe distance.
4. Ask nearby passengers and staff whether the bag belongs to them.
5. Page the owner using the terminal announcement system if the owner is believed to be nearby.

## Cordon and evacuation
- If the owner is not found within 10 minutes, set up a cordon of at least 25 metres around the bag.
- Keep people out of the cordon and away from glass and structural columns.
- Do not use radios or mobile phones within 15 metres of the item.

## Escalation
- Notify the security control room and the duty manager.
- If the bag is suspicious (wires, leaking liquid, unusual smell, owner behaved evasively), call the police bomb disposal unit and begin evacuation planning.
- Record the time found, location, description and who attended.
