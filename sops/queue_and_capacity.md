## Immediate actions
1. Open an additional lane or counter if staff are available. Prioritise the lane with the longest wait.
2. Redeploy queue marshals to speed up divest and tray handling and to keep the line moving.
3. Announce expected wait times so passengers can plan their time.

## Passenger flow
- Direct passengers with imminent departures to a priority or fast-track lane.
- Redirect new arrivals to a less busy checkpoint or entrance if one exists.
- Open overflow queuing space and keep walkways and emergency exits clear.

## Escalation
- If the wait stays above the limit for 15 minutes or the zone is over capacity, inform the duty manager.
- Consider asking airlines to hold check-in cut-offs or delay boarding for affected flights.
- Record peak queue length and wait time for the post-shift review.
