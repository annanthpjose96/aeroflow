## Immediate actions
1. Review the camera view. Long stays are often innocent: delayed passengers, people sleeping, or people waiting for a pick-up.
2. Send a customer-service or security officer to check welfare and offer help.

## Follow-up
- Ask politely whether the person needs assistance, such as directions, rebooking or a place to rest.
- If the person seems unwell, confused or distressed, follow the person-down and welfare checks.
- If behaviour is suspicious or repeats near sensitive areas, inform the security control room.
- Keep a note of the time, place and outcome. Do not record or profile individuals.
