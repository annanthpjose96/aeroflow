## Immediate actions
1. Check the camera view for people pushing, panic or blocked exits.
2. Stop new people entering the area by using barriers or staff at the entrances.
3. Use announcements to ask passengers to move to less crowded areas, giving clear directions.

## Crowd control
- Keep emergency exits and corridors free at all times.
- Move staff to pinch points where crowd flows meet.
- Open additional space or adjacent halls if available.

## Escalation
- If density keeps rising or people appear distressed, alert the duty manager and security control room.
- Prepare first-aid and medical teams for possible crush or fainting incidents.
