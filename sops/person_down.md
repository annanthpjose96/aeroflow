## Immediate actions
1. Send the nearest first-aid responder and call the airport medical team immediately.
2. Check the camera view to see whether the person is moving and whether others are helping.
3. Tell the responder the exact floor position and zone from the alert.
4. Clear a space around the person and keep the crowd back to give them air.

## First response
- Check that the person responds and is breathing. If not breathing, start CPR and send for the defibrillator (AED).
- Do not move the person if a head, neck or back injury is possible.
- Stay with the person and keep them warm and calm until paramedics arrive.

## Escalation
- Call the ambulance service if the person is unconscious, bleeding heavily, in chest pain, or cannot get up.
- Meet the ambulance at the nearest entrance and guide them to the location.
- Log the incident and inform the duty manager.
